PHASE 3 — OPTIONAL COACHING GUIDES v3.4.0

Coverage: 56 programme exercise/alternative entries plus 9 core/reset entries.
Every entry has three written coaching stages and two visual reference positions.
Some static holds, stretches and bike setups intentionally have similar positions.

ILLUSTRATIONS
Purpose-drawn SVG position schematics for the other variations, bundled as visual-*.svg files.
These simplify posture and equipment. They are not anatomical animations or
individual technique assessments. Use the exercise-specific written coaching.

No streaming videos, paid media services, API keys or accounts are required.
