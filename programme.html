Phase 3 · Your complete programme

Version 3.4.0 · Four strength sessions, a reset day, Saturday spin and an optional easy session. Finish your day whenever you need to; skipped sets are never counted as completed work.

MON

LOWER 01

GLUTES + HIP FLEXORS · 40–55 min • express 25–35

Warm-up: Bike 2 min · Hinge drill ×8 · Hip flexion ×8/side

PLANNED

Cable Pull-Through

3 × 10–12 reps · 120 seconds rest

Cable + rope · Stack / kg

Hip hinge · glutes and hamstrings

  1. Set a rope on the lowest pulley. Face away, straddle the rope and step out until the cable is taut.
  2. Soften knees and send hips backwards; hands travel between your thighs. Keep spine comfortable.
  3. Drive through feet and stand tall. Finish upright, without leaning back or pulling with arms.

Cue: Hinge back; squeeze glutes without arching back.

ALTERNATIVE

Dumbbell sumo deadlift

3 × 10–12 reps · 120 seconds rest

1 dumbbell · DB / kg

Hip hinge · glutes and hamstrings

  1. Place one dumbbell upright between your feet. Choose a comfortable wide stance with toes slightly out.
  2. Bend hips and knees to grip it. Keep the weight close and brace gently.
  3. Push the floor away to stand tall. Lower under control; use raised blocks if reaching the floor is uncomfortable.

Cue: Use a comfortable wide stance; hold one dumbbell between your feet. Bend hips and knees, then stand tall without leaning back.

PLANNED

Single-Leg Leg Press

3 × 8–10 reps / side · 120 seconds rest

Machine · Machine / kg

Leg press · thighs and glutes

  1. Adjust the seat so your pelvis stays supported. Place one foot centrally on the platform.
  2. Lower slowly only as far as you can keep your pelvis stable and heel down.
  3. Press through the full foot. Stop short of locking the knee; follow the machine’s safety instructions.

Cue: Keep pelvis level; drive through full foot.

ALTERNATIVE

Bilateral leg press

3 × 8–10 reps · 120 seconds rest

Machine · Machine / kg

Leg press · thighs and glutes

  1. Adjust the seat to support your back and pelvis; place both feet securely on the platform.
  2. Press evenly through both feet, keeping knees in line with toes and avoiding a forceful knee lock.
  3. Bend your knees slowly. Stop the descent before your pelvis lifts or rolls away from the pad.

Cue: Use both feet on the platform. Keep pelvis against the pad, lower with control and press through both feet.

PLANNED

Supported Split Squat

2 × 8–10 reps / side · 120 seconds rest

1 dumbbell · DB / kg

Split squat · thighs and glutes

  1. Use one hand on a stable support; hold a dumbbell in the other. Keep feet in a split stance.
  2. Bend both knees and lower vertically through a comfortable range.
  3. Push through your front foot to rise. Complete both sides; start conservatively.

Cue: Use a stable hand support; lower straight down through a comfortable range.

ALTERNATIVE

Bodyweight split squat

2 × 8–10 reps / side · 120 seconds rest

Bodyweight · Bodyweight / no added load

Split squat · thighs and glutes

  1. Stand in a split stance beside a stable support. Use your hand for balance; no weights are needed.
  2. Bend both knees to lower through a comfortable range, keeping your front foot planted.
  3. Push through your front foot to rise with control. Finish your repetitions, then change sides.

Cue: Hold a stable support. Bend both knees in a split stance; push through the front foot and complete both sides.

PLANNED

Standing Cable Knee Drive

2 × 10–12 reps / side · 60 seconds rest

Cable + ankle strap · Stack / kg

Hip flexion · hip flexors

  1. Attach an ankle strap to a low pulley behind you and hold a stable support.
  2. Lift the working knee forwards without leaning backwards or swinging.
  3. Lower slowly. Keep the range comfortable and stop if the front of the hip pinches.

Cue: Lift knee without leaning back.

ALTERNATIVE

Seated hip flexion

2 × 10–12 reps / side · 60 seconds rest

Ankle weights · Ankle weight / kg

Hip flexion · hip flexors

  1. Secure light ankle weights and sit upright on a firm bench with both feet supported.
  2. Lift one knee slightly while keeping your torso still.
  3. Lower smoothly. Stay unweighted on reset day and avoid hip pinching.

Cue: Sit tall on a firm bench. Lift one knee without leaning back; begin unweighted before using a light ankle weight.

PLANNED

Seated Hip Abduction

2 × 15–20 reps · 60 seconds rest

Machine · Machine / kg

Hip abduction · outer hips

  1. Set the machine pads outside your knees and sit upright with back supported.
  2. Open your knees through a controlled, comfortable range.
  3. Return slowly; do not bounce the stack or rock your trunk.

Cue: Control both directions.

ALTERNATIVE

Standing cable abduction

2 × 15–20 reps / side · 60 seconds rest

Cable + ankle strap · Stack / kg

Hip abduction · outer hips

  1. Use an ankle strap at a low pulley. Stand side-on and hold a stable support.
  2. Move the outer leg sideways a short distance without leaning or turning the pelvis.
  3. Return slowly. Keep the movement small and controlled.

Cue: Hold a support beside a low pulley. Move the working leg sideways without tilting your pelvis, then return slowly.

No bar or Smith bar across hips. Core options in ABS + RESET page.

BACK + UPPER CHEST · 40–55 min • express 25–35

PLANNED

Half-Kneeling 1-Arm Pulldown

Cable · Stack / kg

Vertical pull · back and arms

  1. Kneel securely at a high pulley with a single handle. Use a pad under your knee.
  2. Draw your elbow towards your ribs without twisting or leaning backwards.
  3. Reach back overhead slowly, maintaining a comfortable shoulder range.

Cue: Keep ribs stacked; elbow toward hip.

ALTERNATIVE

Neutral-grip pulldown

3 × 10 reps · 120 seconds rest

Machine / cable · Stack / kg

Vertical pull · back and arms

  1. Fit a neutral-grip handle to the high pulley and secure your thighs under the pad. Sit tall.
  2. Pull the handle towards your upper chest, guiding your elbows down without swinging your torso.
  3. Allow your arms to lengthen slowly overhead. Keep the weight controlled throughout the return.

Cue: Sit with thighs secured, grasp neutral handles and pull elbows towards your ribs. Return overhead without swinging.

PLANNED

Low-to-High Cable Press

3 × 10–12 reps · 120 seconds rest

Dual cables · Each stack / kg

Incline press · chest and shoulders

  1. Set both pulleys low. Take a stable split stance with handles near your lower chest.
  2. Press forwards and slightly upwards without shrugging or arching.
  3. Return with control. Use an incline dumbbell press if two cables are unavailable.

Cue: Press up and forward without shrugging.

ALTERNATIVE

Incline DB press

3 × 10–12 reps · 120 seconds rest

2 dumbbells + bench · Each DB / kg

Incline press · chest and shoulders

  1. Set a low incline bench. Sit with dumbbells supported, then lie back with feet firmly planted.
  2. Press the dumbbells above your upper chest with wrists stacked over your forearms.
  3. Lower slowly with elbows at a comfortable angle. Finish by bringing the weights down safely.

Cue: Set a low incline. Lower dumbbells comfortably towards chest, then press up without arching your back.

PLANNED

Wide-Elbow Chest-Supported Row

3 × 10 reps · 120 seconds rest

2 × dumbbells · Each DB / kg

Row · upper back

  1. Lie chest-down on an incline bench with a dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Row with elbows angled out comfortably; keep chest on the pad.
  3. Lower until arms lengthen without dropping your chest or shrugging.

Cue: Pull elbows outward; keep chest supported.

ALTERNATIVE

Seated cable row

3 × 10 reps · 120 seconds rest

Cable · Stack / kg

Row · upper back

  1. Sit at a low cable row with feet braced and knees softly bent. Take a comfortable handle grip.
  2. Draw the handle towards your torso, moving your elbows back without rocking your trunk.
  3. Let your arms lengthen slowly while keeping your torso steady and the weight controlled.

Cue: Sit tall with feet braced. Pull the handle towards your ribs, keeping torso still; slowly extend arms.

PLANNED

Lean-Away Lateral Raise

2 × 12–15 reps / side · 60 seconds rest

Cable · Stack / kg

Lateral raise · shoulders

  1. Stand beside a low pulley and hold a stable support with your free hand.
  2. Raise the working arm out to the side to a comfortable height, usually below shoulder level.
  3. Lower slowly without swinging. Use a light load and keep your neck relaxed.

Cue: Slow lowering; no swing.

ALTERNATIVE

Machine lateral raise

2 × 12–15 reps · 60 seconds rest

Machine · Machine / kg

Lateral raise · shoulders

  1. Adjust the seat so the pads rest comfortably against your upper arms.
  2. Raise arms out to the sides through a comfortable range.
  3. Lower slowly without bouncing or shrugging.

Cue: Adjust pads comfortably against upper arms. Lift arms out to the sides, then lower without shrugging.

PLANNED

Incline Y Raise

2 × 12 reps · 60 seconds rest

2 light dumbbells · Each DB / kg

Y raise · shoulder control

  1. Lie chest-down on an incline bench with very light dumbbells.
  2. Reach arms forward and out in a Y shape, thumbs pointing upwards.
  3. Lower slowly. Avoid shrugging; use bodyweight if needed.

Cue: Reach up, not shrug.

ALTERNATIVE

Cable Y raise

2 × 12 reps · 60 seconds rest

Dual cables · Each stack / kg

Y raise · shoulder control

  1. Set two light cable handles low. Stand facing the machine with a stable stance and soft knees.
  2. Raise your arms into a comfortable Y shape, keeping your ribs down and avoiding a shrug.
  3. Lower your arms slowly along the same path. Use very light resistance and a comfortable range.

Cue: Use very light low cables. Raise arms forward into a Y without shrugging; return slowly.

Optional abs: dead bug or Pallof press beside cable station.

PLANNED

Bird Dog

2 × 6 reps / side · 60 seconds rest

Bodyweight · Bodyweight / no added load

Core stability · controlled trunk position

  1. Start on hands and knees, with hands below shoulders and knees below hips.
  2. Reach one arm and the opposite leg without arching or rotating.
  3. Pause briefly, return slowly and switch sides.

Cue: Reach opposite arm and leg; keep pelvis level.

ALTERNATIVE

Dead bug

2 × 6 reps / side · 60 seconds rest

Mat · Bodyweight / no added load

Core stability · controlled trunk position

  1. Lie on your back with knees bent above your hips and arms reaching upwards.
  2. Slowly reach one arm and the opposite leg away, keeping your trunk steady and breathing out.
  3. Return to the start and alternate sides. Shorten the reach if your back begins to arch.

Cue: Lie on your back with hips and knees bent. Exhale and extend opposite arm and leg only as far as your trunk stays still.

PLANNED

Tibialis Raise

2 × 15 reps · 60 seconds rest

Bodyweight · Bodyweight / no added load

Ankle movement · shin muscles

  1. Stand with your back supported against a wall and feet slightly forward.
  2. Keep heels on the floor and lift toes towards your shins.
  3. Lower smoothly; bring feet closer to the wall to make it easier.

Cue: Lean on a wall; keep heels down and lift toes.

ALTERNATIVE

Seated toe raise

2 × 15 reps · 60 seconds rest

Bench · Bodyweight / no added load

Ankle movement · shin muscles

  1. Sit on a stable bench with both feet flat on the floor and knees comfortably bent.
  2. Keep your heels on the floor and lift your toes and forefeet towards your shins.
  3. Lower your toes slowly and repeat. Keep your heels still and avoid bouncing.

Cue: Sit with feet flat. Keep heels down while lifting toes; lower slowly.

PLANNED

Open Book

1 × 6 reps / side · 60 seconds rest

Bodyweight · Bodyweight / no added load

Upper-back mobility

  1. Lie on your side with knees bent and arms reaching forwards.
  2. Keep knees together while rotating the top arm and upper back open.
  3. Return gently; do not force the shoulder to touch the floor.

Cue: Keep knees together and rotate gently through upper back.

ALTERNATIVE

Seated thoracic rotation

1 × 6 reps / side · 60 seconds rest

Bench · Bodyweight / no added load

Upper-back mobility

  1. Sit upright on a firm bench with feet planted and arms crossed gently over your chest.
  2. Turn your upper body gently to one side while your hips keep facing forwards.
  3. Return to the centre, then turn to the other side through a comfortable range.

Cue: Sit tall with feet planted. Rotate gently through upper back; keep hips facing forward.

Recovery day: bodyweight only. Finish feeling fresher; skip any movement that provokes symptoms.

Progression and recovery

The app adjusts effort and sets by week. Weeks 1–2: RIR 3. Weeks 3–4: RIR 2–3. Week 5: reduced sets at RIR 4–5. Weeks 6–7: RIR 2. Week 8: RIR 2–3. Loaded hinges remain at RIR 3 outside the deload; reset and optional sessions stay easy. RIR means clean repetitions you could still perform.

Week 5 reduces two- and three-set exercises to one working set. Choose Express in the app to show the first three strength exercises. Rest longer when needed. Complete both sides before the main rest, with extra rest between sides if needed.

Stop painful movements. Persistent or worsening hamstring symptoms need individual assessment. This is general training guidance, not rehabilitation or an assessment of your technique.

After eight weeks the app prompts a review. It does not automatically generate new exercises. A new block repeats the programme and retains history.
